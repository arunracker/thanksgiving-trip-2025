# 🦃 Thanksgiving Adventure 2025 - Austin to Broken Bow

## 📍 Overview
A comprehensive trip planning website for 7 families traveling from Austin, TX to Mt Vernon and Broken Bow, OK for Thanksgiving 2025. This interactive web application provides detailed itineraries, activity options, shopping lists, and coordination tools for a memorable group vacation.

## 🌟 Features

### 📅 Day-by-Day Itinerary
- **Wednesday, Nov 26**: Travel day from Austin to Mt Vernon Mansion
- **Thursday, Nov 27**: Thanksgiving celebration at the mansion
- **Friday, Nov 28**: Adventure day in Broken Bow with multiple activity options
- **Saturday, Nov 29**: Choose between scenic Talimena Drive or relaxation day

### 🎯 Interactive Elements
- **Multiple Activity Options**: Users can choose between different activities based on preferences
  - Friday: Zipline, Maze, or Kayaking adventures
  - Saturday: Scenic mountain drive or lake relaxation
- **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Visual Timeline**: Easy-to-follow schedule with time badges and location markers
- **Smart Navigation**: Bottom navigation bar for quick access to different days

### 🛒 Shopping Coordination
- **Store-by-Store Lists**: Organized shopping divided among families
  - HEB (32 items) - Assigned to Dhanya
  - Costco (11 bulk items) - Assigned to Priya
  - Indian Store (21 items) - Assigned to Roshan
  - Meat/Butcher (5 items) - Assigned to Harsha
  - Per-Family Items - Each family brings their own
- **Complete Prep Schedule**: Monday/Tuesday preparation timeline
- **Visual Checklists**: Interactive checkboxes for tracking purchases

### 🏡 Accommodation Details
- **Mt Vernon Mansion**: 15,000 sq ft luxury estate
  - 9 bedrooms, 11 bathrooms
  - Game room, pool, hot tub
  - Full commercial kitchen
  - Accommodates all 7 families comfortably

### 🚗 Trip Statistics
- **Total Distance**: 800 miles
- **7 Families**: Coordinated travel and activities
- **5 Days**: Wednesday through Sunday adventure

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone or Download** the repository
2. **Open** `index.html` or `index1.html` in any modern web browser
3. **Navigate** using the bottom navigation bar or scroll through the content
4. **Select** your preferred activities for Friday and Saturday
5. **Use** the shopping checklist to coordinate purchases
